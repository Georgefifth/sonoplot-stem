The Accessibility Barrier

The Unseen Wall in STEM Classrooms

Visual curves are intuitive in a fraction of a second for sighted students. For blind learners, standard screen readers hit an impassable barrier.

Before: Current Experience
Silent Alt-Text & Coordinate Dumps

Screen readers announce opaque labels like "Image: curve". Students cannot perceive slopes, turning peaks, or zero crossings without manual human tutoring or waiting weeks for specialized Braille transcribers.

Binaural Sound + Instant Calculus Cards

Continuous pitch sweeps map height (Y), stereo panning maps position (X), earcon chimes sound at roots, and automated text cards report exact extrema, intercepts, and monotonicity intervals.

System Architecture

From Math Formula to Multi-Sensory Perception

Deterministic, zero-latency browser pipeline executing in sub-milliseconds without cloud dependencies or paid APIs.

STAGE 01

Equation / CSV Parser

Parses algebraic formulas f(x) via MathJS AST compiler or accepts raw two-column sensor CSV dumps from Vernier & NIST probes.

Input: y = x³ - 3x | CSV
STAGE 02

Calculus Engine

Numerical central differences evaluate slopes, tangent zero crossings, inflection points, and strictly monotonic intervals.

Roots: ±1.73 | Max/Min
STAGE 03

Spatial Sonifier

Web Audio API sweeps pitch logarithmically, modulates harmonic timbre for negative values, and pans binaural stereo.

Pitch: 220-880Hz | Pan: -1..1
STAGE 04

Tactile & ARIA Output

Emits BANA-compliant ASCII/Braille pin grid matrices and delivers concise, throttled ARIA Live region announcements.

BANA Embosser .txt | ARIA

Open Access Education

Combines continuous audio frequency sweeps, screen-reader speech, and tactile Braille matrices so students are never dependent on sighted human intermediaries.
