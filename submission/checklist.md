# Devpost Submission Checklist — SonoPlot STEM

**Hackathon**: AccessED Hacks  
**Devpost URL**: https://accessed-hacks.devpost.com/  
**Submission Deadline**: November 20, 2026 @ 5:00 PM EST  

---

## 1. Human Action Steps (What You Need to Do)

Follow these steps on Devpost to submit the project:

1. **Log in to Devpost**:
   - Open [https://accessed-hacks.devpost.com/](https://accessed-hacks.devpost.com/) in your browser and log in to your student account.
   - Click **"Enter a submission"** (or edit your existing draft).

2. **Fill General Information**:
   - **Project Name**: Copy from `submission/fields.md` (`SonoPlot STEM`).
   - **Elevator Pitch**: Copy the single-sentence pitch from `submission/fields.md`.

3. **Upload Media Assets**:
   - **Thumbnail / Cover Image**: Upload `submission/assets/thumbnail.png`.
   - **Image Gallery**: Upload all 8 screenshots from `submission/assets/`:
     - `gallery-01-main-explorer.png`
     - `gallery-02-calculus-report.png`
     - `gallery-03-tactile-braille.png`
     - `gallery-04-ear-training-lab.png`
     - `gallery-05-science-csv-data.png`
     - `gallery-06-keyboard-cheatsheet.png`
     - `gallery-07-system-architecture.png`
     - `gallery-08-real-world-validation.png`
   - Copy the corresponding **caption** for each image from `submission/fields.md` (all under 150 characters).

4. **Add Demo Video**:
   - Upload `submission/assets/demo.mp4` to YouTube (set to Unlisted or Public) or attach directly to Devpost.
   - Paste the video URL into the Video field.

5. **Fill "About the Project" Story**:
   - Copy the entire Markdown text from the `About the Project` section in `submission/fields.md` into Devpost's story editor.
   - Verify that headings (`## Inspiration`, `## What it does`, etc.) render cleanly.

6. **Add "Built With" Tags**:
   - Paste the tags from `submission/fields.md`:
     `react, typescript, web-audio-api, accessibility, a11y, screen-reader, braille, bana, sonification, mathjs, calculus, stem, education, vite, tailwind-css, playwright, firefox, vercel`

7. **Add "Try It Out" Links**:
   - **Live Demo / App URL**: `https://accessed-hacks.vercel.app`
   - **GitHub Repository**: `https://github.com/Georgefifth/sonoplot-stem`

8. **Review & Final Submit**:
   - Review against the Pre-Flight Checklist below.
   - Click **"Submit"** before November 20 at 5:00 PM EST.

## 2. Pre-Flight Verification Checklist

Before clicking Submit on Devpost, verify each item:

- [ ] **Title**: "SonoPlot STEM"
- [ ] **Elevator Pitch**: Under 200 characters, clearly explains auditory + tactile math accessibility.
- [ ] **Thumbnail**: High-contrast amber/cyan artwork uploaded (`submission/assets/thumbnail.png`).
- [ ] **Live Demo URL**: Accessible without login at `https://accessed-hacks.vercel.app`.
- [ ] **GitHub URL**: Public repository accessible at `https://github.com/Georgefifth/sonoplot-stem`.
- [ ] **Demo Video**: 1m34s 1080p video with voice narration and synchronized captions uploaded and playing.
- [ ] **Gallery Images**: 8 screenshots uploaded with captions describing real product functionality.
- [ ] **Markdown Formatting**: Headers, bullet points, and LaTeX formulas formatted properly.
- [ ] **No Private Student Data**: Only synthetic/permission-cleared open educational examples used.
- [ ] **All Team Members Added**: Any eligible student team members added to the project.

---

## 3. Post-Submission Verification

Once submitted:
1. Open the public Devpost project URL in an incognito browser window.
2. Verify that the video plays with audio.
3. Verify that the live demo link (`https://accessed-hacks.vercel.app`) opens immediately to the interactive canvas.
4. Verify that the GitHub repository link opens to the clean source code.
